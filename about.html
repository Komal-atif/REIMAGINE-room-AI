About ReImagine Room AI

An AI interior design tool that respects your room and your budget.

Why we built it

Most AI room design tools ignore two things: the real shape of your room and what you can afford. They redraw the walls, add furniture you cannot buy locally and leave you with a picture you cannot use.

ReImagine Room AI starts from your own photo. It keeps the architecture the same, then changes the style, colours and furniture. Prices are in Pakistani rupees and suggestions point to local stores.

Designing a room under budget is the main goal. If you want ideas first, read our small bedroom design ideas or living room ideas for Pakistani homes, then see all the tools on the services page.

What makes it different

Layout-locked

Walls, windows and doors stay where they are in every redesign.

Budget-aware

Set a budget in PKR and the furniture plan is built around it.

Made for local homes

Furniture suggestions reference stores people in Pakistan actually shop from.

Who built it

ReImagine Room AI was built by Komal, Sadaf, Madeeha , software engineering students, using React, TypeScript and Google Gemini. It was created as a university project to explore how AI can make interior design easier to access.
